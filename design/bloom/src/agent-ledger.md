# Agent ledger: Bloom design doc build

One line per catch: the builder rule or gate line that would have prevented it. Every rule applies to every builder.

1. A chain, tally or strip that counts sessions of more than one state colours each bead by its own session's state; arm (needs-you) never marks a session that is not needs-you. (v1 verify: A2 header painted a failed session arm.)
2. Any `.kbd` or secondary glyph inside a filled (primary) control inherits the control's text colour at ≥ .7 opacity; check ≥ 3:1 against the fill in both themes. (v1 verify: ↩ in Create/Lock at ~2:1.)
3. Readable text never uses `--ghost` (disabled tier) or opacity below 1 on a caption-tier token; labels and table cells use `--text-caption` (ink-4) or stronger. (v1 verify: F4 row labels, delta "(new)", C9 rows at 2.5–3:1.)
4. The theme switch keeps a pending target and retargets to the latest click; never compare against `data-theme` to decide whether to switch. (v1 verify: a quick Field→Night double click was dropped.)
5. Interactive demos are driven in a real headless browser before reporting (click every pill, slider and toggle you built; check console), not only screenshotted.
6. A lamp glyph is drawn as one shape over the lamp's own box (a gradient or mask on `inset: 0`), never as a separately positioned, percentage-sized child inside a ring: at 10 px and fractional positions the two snap apart. A builder's "SHELL FIX NEEDED" lands in `lib.css` in the same wave, with the part-local workaround deleted, never left as a scoped override; the gate crops every `.st-done` at DPR 1 and 2 and fails when the core is not centred in the ring. (Bloom 2 verify: done read as a ∩ crescent in colour, motion, window and chat because only form and around carried the fix.)
7. Look at the colour in phone shots, not only at their width: a bead bar is a mask over a flowing layer, and a layer frozen by `?t=` before its first paint drops out under the mask in mobile Chrome, so every 390 shot showed track-only beads. The runtime now freezes `?t=` after two painted frames; a state shown at 390 must show its colour. (Bloom 3: W4 at 390, and round 2's Background beads, were empty.)
8. Copy that states a rule about a token family ("nothing in a bar is cream, navy or wine") is checked against every token in that family by a grep/ΔE script before shipping; an exception the brief allows is stated in the page too. (Round 3 verify: `--sb-house` crests on cream in Night while C7/B21 say no bar does.)
9. When a layout rule takes width from a row (a fraction for the bead bar), list every string the row shows at its narrowest real width and count truncations before and after; any new truncation of a name or state word is reported to the owner as a decision, not shipped silently. (Round 3 verify: 13–15 line-2 state words and most long names newly truncated.)
10. Width-derived component sizes (bead bars snapped to a bead pitch) are computed from the list's column, not from a row's own indent, so every row in one list snaps to the same count; gate: bars in one list share one left edge. (Round 3 verify: C8 rows staggered by one bead.)
11. Changing a width that a grid track depends on (the bead-bar fraction) re-checks every item spanning that track at its max-content width: an item with `width: max-content` in a narrowed span overflows sideways over its neighbours. Gate: no line-2 element of a row overlaps another (probe every visible `.session-row`). (Round 4: at 1/6 the meta block slid over LOGIN, EXIT 1, 138K and the worktree count.)
