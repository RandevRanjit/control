Without a terminal

A chat-first centre over Claude's real TUI. The terminal becomes a tab.

Your wacky idea, drawn. The centre stops being a terminal and becomes the conversation: your prompt opens each turn, every read, write, error and agent sits in line as a card, and Claude's reply streams in under them. Today TRACE repeats the terminal line for line; here each thing is said once. Claude's own TUI keeps running in tmux, untouched, one click away as the terminal tab beside Browser and Git.

It is drawn over option C of the research: a chat view over the real TUI. CONTROL's mod already runs inside every session and already sees every row the conversation keeps, and the reply as it streams; the centre is drawn from that. The composer is CONTROL's own text field, and Send pre-types into Claude's prompt box, then adds the one Enter you pressed. Nothing here is built. The last part of this section says what is still unproven.

X1The window, chat first

The same sessions and the same Claude turn as the window in section 05, with a different centre. The sidebar runs the full height and holds the lights, so the head of the conversation says only what the selected row doesn't: the session's path and, under it, the one full state bar in the window, a single smooth blend inside its state's family that flows on the clock. The lamp, the pearl, the name, the state and the worktree are already on that row, so they aren't repeated. In the sidebar every row carries the same colour through its beads, a sixth of the row, just before the lamp at the far right. Each turn hangs from a thread in the gutter. Your prompt sits on a soft raised pebble, in Plex, because it is exactly what you typed. Every tool call is a pearl on the thread, and Claude's reply reads in the chrome face.

The live turn's thread carries the safe ramp, flowing, and its head is the one marble in the well. When the turn ends, both settle to cream and the session's bar wipes from blue to green, in the head and in its sidebar row together. The panel's tabs are icons: Outputs, Folder, Browser, Git and the terminal. Diff lives inside Git as a mode, Graph | Diff, the way TRACE carries Turns | Diff in the terminal window. TRACE is gone as a tab, because its rows are the conversation now, and the terminal is the small glyph at the end of the strip.

X3Cards at 1:1

One grammar for every kind of row, borrowed from TRACE: a pearl on the turn's thread, the kind in its TRACE colour, the path in Plex, the meta at the right, and a chevron when there is more. A write opens its diff for the live turn and folds in older ones. Consecutive reads fold to one row. A subagent is a session in its own right, so its card carries a tip, three beads in its own state's colour.

Needs-you cards are a different object. They float up from the well on the overlay tone, because they are the one thing in the conversation that waits for you, and the session's bar and lamp turn needs-you while they wait. The first cut only shows the card and leaves the answer to Claude's dialog; the later cut lets your click send the dialog's own key.

X4The pipe, and why not fakechat

You named fakechat, so it was the first thing tested, and it is the wrong pipe for the centre. A channel is an MCP server that Claude Code spawns over stdio. It carries one thing in and one thing out: your message arrives as an outside event (<channel source="plugin:fakechat:fakechat" …>, not your typed turn), and only what Claude passes to the channel's reply tool comes back. fakechat's own instructions to Claude say it plainly: "Anything you want them to see must go through the reply tool — your transcript output never reaches the UI" (server.ts:63). The work, which is what you want in the centre, never crosses it.

CONTROL already ships the pipe that does carry the work. Its mod runs inside every Claude session, and the mod API has session.append, which "fires once per row a conversation of this session keeps (a prompt, a response block, a tool result, a notice), before it is stored" (index.d.ts:4196-4207), and turn.step, which streams the response chunk by chunk (:1135-1139). That is the whole chat, live, with no file tailing.
