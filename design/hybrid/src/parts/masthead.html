T3's engine, in Bloom.

CONTROL v2 is a hard fork of T3 Code: its server, its threads, its chat view, its panels. Bloom is the look you confirmed four rounds ago: Field and Night, Neue Regrade and Plex, colourless lamps, state colour flowing through bead bars, and a centre that is the conversation rather than a terminal. This sheet asks where each side should win.
