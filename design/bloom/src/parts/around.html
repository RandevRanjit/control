Around the window

Menu bar, lock, icon, sheets and notes, with pearls and state bars inside them.

The menu-bar icon stays a still template image: state by shape. The panel under it gets pearls and bead bars: one pearl per session in its header, and on every row a short bar of beads with the colour flowing through them, just before the lamp. The lock screen is the one CONTROL screen where nothing needs you, so it is the one place the whole ramp is allowed to move.

A1Menu-bar icon

macOS draws a template image in one ink and recolours it for each bar, so the icon carries no colour, and it carries no motion either. The stair holds every state by shape alone: the apex step fills for working, a check in the right shoulder marks done, and the whole canvas reverses for needs you. Bloom changes two things. Strokes take round caps and round joins, like the mark. And done's filled corner square becomes a check, because Bloom 2 drops every corner mark for done: a square or dot in a corner was the half-moon's grammar.

A2The menu-bar panel

The panel is 360 wide and floats, so it takes the overlay tone, the navy float shadow and a lip of light on its top edge. Its header line is three pearl tallies, one pearl per session in that session's own state colour, each with its count beside it: the mix reads at a glance, and the number is there without a hover. Every row is the sidebar's row at the panel's width: the name on the first line, then a bead bar a quarter of the panel wide, then the lamp at the far right as its end cap, 15 px. The second line holds Claude's spark and the state words, then the context line, the tokens the chat has spent and the age. The session that needs you keeps its tint and takes a third line for Go to tab. Lamps and bars run on the window's clock, so the needs-you beads here flow in step with the ones in the sidebar.

A3The lock screen

A full lock is a commitment: no early unlock. It is also the one CONTROL screen where nothing needs you, so it is the one place the whole ramp may move. The shut stair is 30 beads with the full ramp laid once around it. It turns once every 14 s, the slowest loop in the app, and each bead breathes on the same 14 s as the colour passes through it. Nothing else moves.

When a session needs you, only that session opens. The window comes to the front without key focus, its band carries the lamp, the note and the session's full state bar, and the lock mark drops to the safe ramp, so the needs-you bar is the only arm in view.

A4App icon

The icon is the only other place the full ramp appears, and it does not move. The bead stair rides 3 % of the body above centre on Night's grey field, #2F2E2E, where the plateaus read cleanly against no hue at all; at 64 and 32 px the beads close up into one coloured stair.

A5Sheets and the ⌘K finder

Sheets float at radius 20 with the navy float shadow. Fields and wells sink, buttons are pills, and the selected segment slides. One primary per sheet, in ink. Danger text appears once, on Archive anyway?, because archiving ends a session mid-turn. The lock sheet's no-early-unlock sentence is a promise, not a danger, so it stays in ink.
