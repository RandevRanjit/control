Form and type

Soft forms, no hairlines, Neue Regrade for the chrome.

Edges come from temperature and tone, not strokes. A raised thing separates from its ground by a small step in lightness and a warm lip; a sunk thing by a cool navy shadow on its top edge. Nothing is outlined, and nothing casts a grey shadow.

F1The softness scale

Eight radii, and each belongs to one kind of object. The smaller the thing, the tighter its corner, so a keycap still looks exact beside a pane and a row still lines up with the terminal. Anything you press or switch is a pill. The 28 px corner exists only for showcase objects on this sheet and never reaches the app.

F2Edges without lines

Direction D parted regions with 1 px rules and square corners. Bloom parts them with tone: the chrome is one small step lighter than the field, wells one step darker, and regions sit apart on 6 pt gutters. In Night the field, the chrome and the wells are one neutral grey: the chrome steps up from #2F2E2E, the wells step down to #1B1A1A, and nothing carries a blue cast, so the field stays out of the way of the state colours. Where two things share a tone and still need parting, a 2 pt rounded seam does it. That seam is the only line in the window.

F3The inflated material

Bloom shades its objects toward navy, never grey. On the light field that gives a dark silhouette for free. On Night's grey field, #2F2E2E, the navy shading lands close to the field's own value and the edge goes soft. A cream rim at the silhouette brings it back. v1's 0.45 now reaches only 2.31–2.69:1 against the field. At 0.60 it reaches 3.44–3.80:1 on the field and 3.01–3.33:1 on raised chrome, so 0.60 is not taste: it is the smallest rim that gives every silhouette the 3:1 graphics need, field and chrome alike. Inside a well it reaches 4.41–4.87:1. Field keeps 0.30, which softens its edge.

The pearl is the same material made for chrome, at 8 to 20 px: one still element, no pseudo-element, so a sidebar of them costs nothing. It carries a group's identity beside its name, a session's state in a tally, and a count. Under 10 px it drops the lift and keeps the rim and the occlusion, so it stays crisp. A pearl never carries text, except the neutral count pearl.

F4Two chrome faces and Plex

Chrome is set in Neue Regrade, your pick, and content stays in Plex. Rounded (Nunito, standing in for SF Pro Rounded) stays in the first column only as the comparison it lost. Plex keeps everything you could paste into a shell. The three columns below stay fixed whatever the nav says; the nav's Regrade | Rounded toggle still swaps every chrome string on the page if you want to look again.

F5Glyphs and the mark

D drew its glyphs on a 16 grid with square caps and mitred joins, ORBIT's orthogonal law. Bloom keeps the grid and the paths and rounds every cap and join, so the set matches the soft forms without changing what any glyph says. Five glyphs are new, for a centre without a terminal: term, send, stop, clip and diff. A sixth, spark, is Claude's own mark: it stands in for the word claude on every session row. The stair mark gets the same treatment.

The lamps are glyphs too, and in Bloom 2 they are ink only: shape and motion say the state, and the bead bar just before them carries the colour. Done is a tick and it holds still; failed is a cross that stumbles. The half-moon is gone: v1 set a dot in the ring's corner, and at row size that read as a crescent.
