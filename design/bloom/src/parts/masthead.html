CONTROL in Bloom.

A redraw of CONTROL's look after one motion loop: Bloom, an inflated flower whose colour flows through it every 7 seconds. This sheet borrows the loop's system, not its flower: a cool field that is the blue family turned down, three softened primaries that only meet through cream or navy, inflated forms shaded with navy instead of grey, and every loop a power of two of 7 seconds. Field is the default. Night is the same system with the lights off, on a plain near-black grey.

Bloom 2 moves the colour out of the lamps and into a row of flowing beads beside each session's lamp: blue working, red needs you, amber failed, green done. The lamps keep their motion and shape, in ink. The pearls and ramps that stayed on the showcase now sit inside the app, and one section tries a centre without a terminal.

Changes

  • State colour lives in one place: each session's bead bar, the state's colour as one smooth ramp inside its own family, flowing through a row of beads on the clock. It takes a sixth of the row at the right, just before the lamp, and a quarter in the menu-bar panel; working and Background share it. The session in focus gets the one continuous bar, at the top. working needs you failed done
  • Lamps carry no hue. Each state keeps its own motion and shape in ink, so the lamp says how urgent and the bar says which state. In rows and the menu-bar panel the lamp is 15 px, the row's end cap.
  • Each row's second line carries Claude's spark, a small context line with its %, the tokens the chat has spent, and the age.
  • Usage, 5H and 7D, is a thin line in plain ink: no hue and no beads.
  • Pearls in the app: the bead material at 8–20 px for group identity, tallies and counts.
  • Field on the reel's own #D6DEEE is the default. Night is one neutral grey: the field #2F2E2E, wells #1B1A1A and inputs #242323 below it. Its one blue-black is only ever shading.
  • Soft forms: radius, inflated controls, no hairlines. Motion on one clock: 1.75, 3.5, 7 and 14 s, phase-locked across the window.
  • Neue Regrade for the chrome; Plex stays for everything you could paste into a shell.
  • One idea to try: a centre without a terminal. The session reads as a conversation, and the terminal is one tab away.

Stays

  • The work is the hero and the chrome stays calm, whether the centre is the terminal or the conversation.
  • Only arm red means needs you; danger means destructive. Failed is amber, well clear of arm.
  • State reads by shape first, then motion, then colour.
  • Reduce motion stills every loop, the bars included.
  • The menu-bar icon is a still template image.
  • CONTROL never presses Enter for you and never posts a banner.
