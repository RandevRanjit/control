Lock

The full lock's four surfaces in version 1: the offer, the sheet, the plate and terminal claude's refusal. Drawn 11 Oct 2026 for the owner's approval.

A lock is a commitment device, not security. It ends at a fixed time and nothing lifts it early. A full lock refuses your sends in every chat, from every device, and refuses new prompts in terminal claude; running turns and agents carry on, and a chat that needs you comes through until you've answered it. A group lock does the same for the groups you tick. Bloom drew v1's lock for the menu bar and tmux (design/bloom, section 07); version 1 has neither, so the surfaces move into the window and into Claude Code's own prompt.

L1The offer

When nothing has needed you for 10 minutes, a note offers a lock in the update note's slot, under the search. Nothing pops: it waits for your next look, which is the moment it's for. Lock opens the sheet set to Full for 2 h, and only the sheet's Lock commits. Later hides the note until something next needs you. It never shows during a lock. A failed chat doesn't count as needing you; an idle one doesn't stop the offer.

L2The lock sheet, ⌘L

⌘L opens the sheet, and so do the offer's Lock and Lock… in a group's menu, which ticks that group. It is the only way in, so the promise is read at the moment it matters. Full covers the window, every chat and terminal claude. Group covers the groups you tick: they start as the groups with a working chat, less the one you're in. For is 30 min, 1 h or 2 h, or a time typed into Until, at most 12 h ahead; the end is fixed the moment you press Lock.

The no-early-unlock sentence is a promise, not a danger, so it is in ink and Lock is the primary. During a lock, ⌘L can only extend it: the presets that would end sooner go, and Lock reads Extend.

L3The lock plate

During a full lock the window is the plate: the logo, the end time, what is still running and one sentence, on white in Field and black in Night. There is no sidebar, panel or composer to get past, and quitting and relaunching lands on it again. The logo is the sidebar's stair drawn in 5 px cells, and a band of shadow crosses it in a 4 × 4 ordered dither, left to right on the 7 s clock, so most of the logo is lit at any moment and the shadow's cells hold at 22 %. Under Reduce motion it holds still and whole. ⌘L on the plate opens the sheet to extend.

L3bBreak-through

When a chat needs you, the plate steps aside for it. The window comes forward without taking focus, so typing in another app can't answer it, and holds only the chats that need you, under a Locked until note in the offer's slot. The chat is open, and you answer in the composer as always. There is no search, panel, settings, fold or usage. Once nothing needs you, the plate comes back. A failure doesn't come through; a question or an approval does.

L4Terminal claude

Terminal claude learns of the lock from a UserPromptSubmit hook in ~/.claude/settings.json, which reads CONTROL's lock file. A refused prompt stays in the box, and Claude Code prints the hook's line in its warning colour. claude -p is refused the same way. CONTROL's own chats pass the hook, because the server already refuses your sends to them and must let agents' messages through. Running turns and permission prompts are untouched: the hook only sees new prompts. Past midnight the time reads 10:00 tomorrow.
