The window

Main window in both themes, ARCHIVE and the diff, rows at 1:1.

A redraw, not a reskin. The sidebar now runs the full height of the window and holds the traffic lights, so the band belongs to the session you selected: its group's pearl, its name, and under the name the one full state bar in the window, flowing over the terminal. Every row ends in its own bead bar and lamp, every group has its pearl, and usage is a plain line of ink. Lamps keep their shapes and motions and lose their colour. The terminal is still the centre, in a deep well; the next section, Without a terminal, takes it out.

W1Field

Field is the default. The chrome is one tone step above the field (1.11:1) and the terminal sits on the field itself, in a soft well, with navy ink at 9.84:1. Colour lives in the bars, the pearls and the live turn's rail, and inside the wells in the kind labels and the diff; everything else is the field, the chrome and ink. ORBIT-WEBSITE is selected, so its working bar runs 6 px tall and unbroken in the band, and as a row of 5 px beads in its row, both on the 7 s working period. Each bar is one smooth blend inside its own family, so its colour flows rather than steps. STATISTICS's bar is the only arm on screen and, with its lamp, the fastest thing on it: one period every 1.75 s.

TRACE streams T14 on the shared clock: seven events between 0.9 s and 8.8 s, the turn marks done at 10 s, and the stream starts again every 14 s. Its turns hang on a rail of pearls, and the live turn's rail is the safe ramp, so it can flow right beside state.

W2Night, on #2F2E2E

Night's field is a plain near-black grey, #2F2E2E, with a chroma of 0.001. The chrome steps up from it in the same grey (1.14:1) and everything sunk steps down in it: the terminal, TRACE and OUTPUTS sit in #1B1A1A wells, 1.28:1 below the field, and the tabs' track, the usage track and the diff gutters in #242323, 1.16:1 below. No surface carries a hue, so nothing pulls against the blue, green and amber bars; the only blue-black left is the shading under raised things. Ink-1 is 11.53:1 on the field and 14.79:1 in the well.

Every pearl carries a stronger cream rim in Night, mixed at 0.60: the smallest rim that gives a pearl's silhouette 3:1 on the field (3.44–3.80) and on the chrome (3.01–3.33).

W3ARCHIVE open, with the diff

ARCHIVE opens under the sessions: search, sort, and 412 finished sessions and chats, the count in a pearl. Three rows are selected, so the selection note has risen at the sidebar's foot, inside its own region. On the right, TRACE is in Diff mode: the same writes as one diff against HEAD, in the well. Night's well is a neutral #1B1A1A and the two washes sit either side of it: removed lines sink 0.049 below it in lightness and added lines rise 0.056 above it. In Field they move −0.037 and +0.030. Long lines wrap under their code.

W4Rows at 1:1

Every state and every interaction at its real size: rows 42, groups 28, worktree rows 22. Read a row from the right. The lamp is the end cap, 15 px, ink, and it only moves. Before it, a bead bar a sixth of the row wide carries the colour, flowing through its beads. The name has the rest of the first line. Needs you flows fastest, one period every 1.75 s at 68.6 px/s. Failed and done take 3.5 s at 51.4 px/s; working, background and choose 7 s at 34.3 px/s; housekeeping 14 s on the safe ramp. Outside, idle and gone hold still.

The second line starts with Claude's spark and the state words, then any chip. Under the bar sit the context line and its share, the tokens this chat has spent, and the age, so every column lines up down the list. A needs-you row also takes a 6 % arm tint and a semibold name, and ink-1 on it is 9.89:1 in Field and 9.58:1 in Night. Working and background share one bar; the lamp tells them apart, a disc for one job and a ring for several, and the wave after the state words counts them. Outside's beads are open rings, like its lamp, and its row has no spark: it is not Claude's. Idle and gone show only the track. A folded group swaps its rows for a tally: one pearl per hidden session, each in its own session's colour. Field's amber bar is only 1.62:1 on the chrome, so it reads by hue, while the lamp's stumble and the word failed carry it for everyone.

W5The band without the sidebar

Hide the sidebar (⌘B) and the band takes over the session's identity: the traffic lights, the lamp, the group's pearl, the name and the full bar. It is the one place a lamp sits in the band. Pick a state: the lamp changes shape and motion, and both bars are wiped off by their bridge while the new ramp already flows underneath, in 437.5 ms, or 218.75 ms into needs you. A bar blends inside its own family; a change crosses families, so it bridges: cream, except between a calm state and needs you, where it is navy and wine. With Still on, the change dissolves in 218.75 ms instead.
